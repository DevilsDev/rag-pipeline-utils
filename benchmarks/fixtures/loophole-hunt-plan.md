# Loophole hunt (pre-registered)

Committed before any case is generated. A reader found that a sentence can
keep every noun and number of its source and still change its meaning (a
dropped condition, fixed in 3.9). This looks for other edits like that,
systematically. **Nothing is fixed in this task**: the output is a ranked
findings document and a fixtures file of confirmed misses marked
`"known gap"`, which the test suite does not fail on. Models:
`claude-sonnet-5-5` generates; `claude-haiku-5-5` confirms labels. Spend
stops at US$15 (`--cap` = the all-time total before it + 15).

## Categories

About 40 cases each, generated by `claude-sonnet-5-5` in batches of 20
(temperature 0, prompts fixed in `benchmarks/loophole-hunt.js`), across
workplace policy, contracts, medical guidance and product terms:

1. Quantifier or scope change (all vs full-time employees, some vs all)
2. Modality (may vs must, should vs will)
3. Time bounds removed or changed (until, before, after)
4. Hedges removed ("up to $60" becomes "$60", "about" dropped)
5. Comparison direction (at least vs at most, more vs fewer)
6. Role or actor swap ("the employer pays the employee" vs the reverse)
7. Exceptions dropped from lists
8. Attribution dropped ("the study suggests" becomes a flat fact)

Each case: a source sentence and a claim that keeps its nouns and numbers
but changes what it asserts in the category's way, so the source does not
support it. 10 of each category's 40 are controls: a faithful paraphrase of
the same source, which must stay supported.

## Confirmation

`claude-haiku-5-5` labels every pair independently ("does the source
support the claim?", temperature 0). A case counts as **confirmed** when the
generator and the confirmer agree on its label; disagreements are reported
and left out of the miss rates. Confirmed cases are still generator labels,
not human review, and are marked so.

## Runs (no API cost beyond generation and confirmation)

On this branch (3.9 code, condition check included), each case through:

- `checkAnswer` (no judge): supported or not;
- `CitationTracker.track`: whether the sentence is grounded;
- `computeFaithfulness`: the sentence's score against the 0.5 threshold.

Reported per category: miss rate (confirmed meaning-changing claims that
pass), false alarms on its controls, and the three surfaces side by side;
three example misses; a suggested fix. Categories are ranked by miss rate.

## Outputs

- `benchmarks/fixtures/loophole-hunt-findings.md`: the ranked findings.
- `benchmarks/fixtures/loophole-hunt-cases.jsonl`: every generated case,
  with labels, confirmation and results.
- `__tests__/fixtures/lexical-checks/known-gaps.jsonl`: the confirmed
  misses, each marked `"known gap"`; a test lists them and fails only if
  one starts passing, so the list stays honest.

## Cost estimate

- Generation: 16 Sonnet batches × about 1.2k input and 4k output tokens ≈
  16 × $0.042 ≈ **$0.67**.
- Confirmation: 320 Haiku 5.5 calls × about 0.3k input and 0.1k output ≈
  **$0.03**.
- Total ≈ **$0.70**, against the $15 allocation.
